Support

DrCanI identifies a property you point your phone camera at and shows its sale history, value estimate and neighborhood data.

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Email us at swefootgames@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 3 business days. Please include your device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened.

Frequently asked questions

How does DrCanI find the house I am pointing at?

The app combines your GPS position and compass heading to estimate the address about 15 meters in front of you, and reads any visible house number in the photo to confirm it. If it is unsure, it shows a confidence level and lets you pick a nearby property.

The wrong house or no data was shown. Why?

GPS accuracy varies, especially between closely spaced houses. Stand on the street in front of the house, keep it centered in the frame and try again. Some properties have no public sale data. Tap "Not the right house?" to choose a neighbor.

Can I use a photo from my gallery?

Yes, if the photo contains location (GPS) metadata. Photos without location data cannot be used.

Why does the app need location, camera and photo access?

Location is used to identify the property, the camera to take the photo, and photo library access only if you choose to upload an existing picture.

How accurate is the property data?

Data comes from third-party real estate data providers and public records and may be incomplete or out of date. Value estimates are not appraisals. Do not rely on DrCanI as the sole basis for a financial decision.

Is DrCanI free?

Yes. There are currently no paid features or subscriptions. Signed-in users have a usage limit on property lookups.

How do I delete my account?

Open the profile menu in the app and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and associated data. If you cannot access the app, email swefootgames@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what DrCanI collects, why, and your choices. Contact: swefootgames@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Location: your precise GPS position and compass heading at the moment you capture or look up a property.
  • Photos: photos you take or select are analyzed on your device (building and house-number detection). They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Account information: if you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address (or Apple's private relay address) and a unique user ID. Guests can use the app without an account.
  • Usage data: the coordinates or address of lookups you perform and a lookup count, used to provide the service and enforce usage limits.

How we use it

To identify the property, retrieve its data, authenticate you, enforce usage limits and keep the service working. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising or track you across other apps and websites.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase: hosting, authentication and our backend.
  • Google: sign-in and map display (Google Maps).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple.
  • Real estate data providers: the address of a lookup is sent to retrieve sale and property data.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not share your personal data with anyone else except where required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and lookup data until you delete your account. You can delete your account in the app (profile menu, Delete account) or by emailing us. Deletion removes your account and associated lookup history.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke location, camera and photo permissions in your device settings, although the app cannot identify properties without location. You can request access to or deletion of your data at any time by email.

Children

DrCanI is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.
